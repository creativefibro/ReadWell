# Glossary

One name per idea, used in every button, title, message and test.

## Voice
Calm, warm, brief. Talk to Susan as "you". Plain words; no urgency, scores or guilt.
On shared screens use device-neutral verbs ("choose", "open"), never "click" or "tap".

## Terms
| Term | Means | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ReadWell | The app | the reader, the system |
| Queue | Articles saved and not yet finished | list, inbox, backlog, library |
| Article | One saved web page in the Queue | item, entry, bookmark, link |
| Opens in Medium / Opens in original | An article ReadWell does not reproduce; reading it opens the original page or app | external, link-only (code word only) |
| Save | Add an article to the Queue | add, clip, capture (code word only) |
| Already in your Queue | The article was saved before | duplicate |
| Continue Reading | The article most recently opened and not finished | resume, in progress |
| Lucky Dip | Open a randomly chosen article from the Queue | random, shuffle, surprise me |
| Oldest first / Newest first / Shortest first | Queue order by date saved or reading length | sort by date |
| Reading length | Estimated minutes to read, shown as "8 min read" | duration, time |
| Reader | ReadWell's calm view of an article | viewer, preview |
| Finish | Say you have read an article and decide what happens to it | complete, done, mark read |
| Rating | Optional one to five stars for how much you enjoyed or gained from it | score, grade |
| Delete | Remove the article for good; its rating and history stay | remove, trash, discard |
| History | Record of finished articles, kept after the article is gone | log, stats |
| Highlight | Marked passage in one of the colours (M3) | annotation, mark |
| Note | Your words attached to a Highlight (M3) | comment, annotation |
| Save for Offline | Keep an article readable without a connection (M3) | download, cache |
| Export | Send a finished article to Obsidian or Capacities (M4) | sync, publish |
| Archive to Anybox | Keep a finished article without highlights in Anybox (M4) | bookmark, store |
| Reading Insights | Quiet record of what you have read (M5) | stats, dashboard, analytics |
